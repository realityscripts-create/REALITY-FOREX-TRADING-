# Reality FX Academy — The Badges Letter

Draft email to send to every enrolled student (and to show on the Academy's
welcome/registration flow so students know what they're aiming for before they
start).

---

**Subject: You're not just learning to trade — you're earning your place**

Dear Trader,

Welcome inside Reality FX OS. Before you take your first lesson, we want you to
know what this academy truly rewards — because it is not what most courses
reward.

Most courses give you a certificate for showing up. We don't. Reality FX OS has
a badge system, and it is deliberately rare. Only five badges exist, and every
one of them must be **earned** — nobody gets one for merely passing, and
nobody gets one for 73%. They are the proof of the fights you didn't give up
on.

**The Five Badges**

⚔️ **First Blood** — passed your very first chapter quiz. Every master started
exactly here. It costs you nothing but your first honest attempt.

🎖️ **Honours** — scored 80% or higher on a chapter quiz. Not everyone reaches
the recognition line — when you do, we notice. This is the badge of consistent
excellence, and it is the first rung of the ladder that matters.

💎 **Flawless** — scored 100% on a chapter quiz. No redos, no second chances
taken: clean execution, clean thinking. This is the badge of the student who
reads like a professional.

🦁 **Heart of a Lion** — failed a chapter, walked through the reflection
period, came back, and passed it. The market will knock you down eventually;
this badge proves you already know how to get back up. In trading, that is not
a soft skill. That is the skill.

🏆 **Distinction Hunter** — took a chapter you'd already passed, and pushed
past 90% because a pass was not enough for you. This is the badge of the
student who refuses to be average. Excellence is a habit, and you chose it.

And when you land 80%+ on two chapters in a row, the Academy knows — the
distinction streak is tracked, celebrated, and shown on your dashboard. It is
your own momentum, made visible.

**Why badges matter**

Think of a badge as being named top of the class. In a school of hundreds, the
students who hold these badges are the ones who wanted it more — and in
trading, desire without discipline is just expensive hope. The badges are our
way of honouring the discipline before the profit, because the discipline is
what produces the profit.**What badge-holders get**

Students who earn badges earn more than recognition — they earn the **Academy Vault**. Earn two badges and the Vault opens: founder-level advanced lessons, and a place in the **Recognition Circle**, the Academy's private network of top performers.

Your badges also travel with you — they appear on your certificate, so anyone who sees your qualification sees the standard you held yourself to. And for those who pursue employment after graduating: Recognition Circle members hold an express pass to the Reality FX board. It is rare — the course proves you are the one for the job; the interview confirms you fit our system. But it is real, and it is yours to reach for.

And one honest note, because we owe you honesty: a certificate from Reality FX
means something *because* of these standards. When you graduate with your
verified certificate, you will not be one of the thousands of traders who
"kind of understand" — you will be in a class of your own, because you earned
it the only way it can be earned: with your own mind, your own hours, and your
own refusal to settle.

Your journey starts at Chapter 1. The first badge is waiting.

Trade like it matters. Because it does.

— The Reality FX Academy

Trading

Trading roles are performance-based, not salary-based. Reality FX allocates capital within strict risk parameters, and compensation is a share of risk-adjusted performance — measured on profitability, risk management, consistency, maximum drawdown, rule adherence and capital preservation. When the trader wins, Reality FX wins. The ladder: Apprentice → Junior → Funded → Senior → Portfolio.

Terms & Conditions

Business Transparency & Compliance Statement

Reality FX operates as a transparent education and mentorship provider. We are committed to honesty in how we market, price, and deliver our services. Every program detail — what is included, what is not, and what you can reasonably expect — is stated clearly before you purchase.

Commitment to Ethical Practices

We hold ourselves to the highest ethical standards. We never guarantee profits, never pressure you into decisions, and never sell you something that is not in your interest. Our mentorship is educational guidance, not financial advice — and we will always tell you the difference.

Transparency of Services

You will always know exactly what you are paying for: the curriculum, the mentorship hours, the duration of access, and any additional resources. If a program changes, we will tell you before you commit — never after.

User Responsibility

You are responsible for the decisions you make with your own capital. We provide the education, tools, and discipline framework; applying them consistently is your part of the partnership. Trading involves risk, and no educational program can remove that risk — it can only prepare you to manage it.

No Guarantee of Results

Results depend on your effort, consistency, capital, and the markets themselves. Past performance — ours or any trader's — is not a promise of future results. Anyone who guarantees trading income is not being honest with you; we will never be that person.

Refund Policy

The Honest Guarantee

We are not here to take your money and disappear. If you complete the first chapter of any course and genuinely feel it is not for you, email us within 7 days of purchase and we will refund you in full — no questions, no friction, no hard feelings. We would rather earn a client for life than keep a fee from someone who felt misled.

Why Chapter One Is Deliberately Light

Chapter 1 is your orientation — a gentle introduction that lays the groundwork in plain language. The real depth arrives in Chapter 2 and beyond: the precise entry models, risk mathematics, live session breakdowns, and the discipline frameworks that take time to build properly. We tell you this upfront so the first chapter never feels like a bait-and-switch.

See the Depth Before You Commit

Because we never want you buying on faith, the full curriculum outline — every chapter, module, and lesson — is available to you before purchase. Review what Chapters 2 to 13 actually contain and judge the level of detail for yourself before spending a cent. Contact us for a guided preview of any later chapter before you buy; we are happy to show you the depth.

After Chapter One

Once you have completed the first chapter and continued into the full program, refunds no longer apply — by then you have seen the depth, the value, and the standard of teaching, and full curriculum access has been delivered. If you are ever unhappy with your experience, contact us anyway: we resolve issues fairly, case by case, because a single unhappy student is a cost we never want to carry.

Our mentors publish high-probability trade setups across forex, commodities, and indices. Each signal includes:

  • Exact entry price and order type

  • Market context and reasoning

Available exclusively to Silver, Gold, and Platinum members. Signals are published during active market hours with 24/5 coverage.

Service 05

Risk Management

The difference between a trader who survives and one who disappears is not strategy — it is risk management. At Reality FX, we treat capital preservation as sacred. Every mentorship session, every signal, every piece of advice is filtered through the lens of protecting your account first.

Our risk framework covers:

  • Position sizing methodology tailored to your account
  • Maximum risk per trade (typically 1–2% of account)
  • Portfolio correlation analysis
  • Emotional management protocols — how to trade without fear or greed
  • Drawdown recovery strategies
  • Calculated risk-to-reward ratios on every setup

This is woven into every tier of our program. From your first lesson to your final mentorship session, risk management is not an afterthought — it is the foundation.

Service 06

Learn & Earn

Theory without application is just entertainment. Our Learn & Earn program bridges the gap between knowledge and execution by helping you develop your own personal trading plan — one that aligns with your lifestyle, your goals, and your personality.

Your trading plan will include:

  • Your defined trading sessions (when you will trade, when you will not)
  • Your specific setup criteria (exactly what conditions must be met)
  • Your risk parameters (how much you risk, how you size positions)
  • Your journaling framework (what you track, how you review)
  • Your emotional checkpoints (how you handle wins, losses, and drawdowns)
  • Your accountability structure (how your mentor keeps you on track)

By the end of this process, you will not just know how to trade — you will know how you trade. And that is the edge that separates hobbyists from professionals.
